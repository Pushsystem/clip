# 5 หมูตัวโต — Treatment

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), sixth film in the same series. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 38.4 s, for TikTok. Thai voice-over (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural), Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of "5 สายพันธุ์หมูที่ใหญ่ที่สุดในโลก" (Tongfunfact), retold in our own words; its captions could not be fetched. Breed weights differ widely between sources, so the film gives each breed's defining look and origin rather than a weight; the only hard numbers are the Guinness record (FACTS.md).

## Structures considered
1. A size chart of five pigs side by side: unreliable numbers would be the whole film.
2. A record-only film about Big Bill: misses the topic.
3. **Chosen**: a countdown 5 → 1 (medallion per rank, each pig trotting in with its feature in a call-out), then the record holder weighed on a balance against a car, and an ending that weighs an ordinary big boar against four people.
