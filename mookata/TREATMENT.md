# หมูกระทะมาจากไหน — Treatment

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), the same series look as "หมูมาเอง". Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 38.5 s, for TikTok. Thai voice-over (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural) and Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of the short "ต้นกำเนิด หมูกระทะ #เล่าอดีต1นาที" (AUNZA THAILAND), retold in our own words. Its captions could not be fetched. The origin of mookata is not settled, so the film tells the legends as legends (FACTS.md).

## Structures considered
1. A single origin story told as fact: rejected, the sources disagree.
2. A do/don't split, "myth vs fact": too little hard fact to fill it.
3. **Chosen**: three numbered legends (Mongol helmet → Japanese "Genghis Khan" dome → "Korean grilled beef" sign), a "?" turn, then the three things the Thai version added, set out as a recipe card (soup moat → fat drips into the soup → suki dipping sauce), then the top view of the grill as an atomic-age pattern, ending on "สูตรของคนไทย" ✓.
