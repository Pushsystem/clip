# ทำไมทั่วโลกมีมังกร — Treatment

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), fourth film in the same series. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 37.8 s, for TikTok. Thai voice-over (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural), Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of "ทำไมทั่วโลกถึงมีตำนานมังกร" (Point of View), retold in our own words; its captions could not be fetched (FACTS.md).

## Structures considered
1. A world tour of dragons, one culture per card: pretty, but it doesn't answer "why".
2. Myth vs. science split: too binary; the theories are theories.
3. **Chosen**: a classroom lesson with three numbered theories (fossil bones → fear of predators → untamable nature), ending on the idea they share: dragons are a fear the whole world has in common.
