Requested style: Text Flash Cuts (✦). That style draws its characters with an image generator, which this machine does not have (no Codex CLI; Canva images could not be downloaded at full size). The user then asked for a pig to tell the story, and chose **the pig narrating in the Mid-century Cartoon style** (`midcentury-toon`), the same series look as the four earlier films; everything is drawn in code.
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 42.4 s, for TikTok. Thai voice-over in the first person as the pig (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural, pitched up), Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of "กลลวงอาหารเช้าแสนล้าน! ... เบคอน" (KhunBomb), retold in our own words; its captions could not be fetched (FACTS.md).

## Structures considered
1. A straight history of bacon: long, and the twist is lost.
2. Myth vs. truth split: there is little myth to split.
3. **Chosen**: the pig as an unlikely narrator of how people were persuaded to eat it every morning, told as a classroom "plan in three steps" (ask the doctors → doctors say yes → the front page), a lid-lift reveal, and a punchline: the pig goes to hide.
