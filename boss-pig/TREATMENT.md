# ถ้าคุณเกิดเป็นหมู — Treatment

The user supplied five pictures of their boss-pig mascot (sunglasses, gold chain, suit or overalls, in a home-goods store) and asked for a storytelling cartoon with this pig. The pictures are the user's own material and are used as they are; everything around them (camera moves, step headers, tags, call-outs, medallion irises, colour-block wipe, caption cards) is drawn with the Mid-century Cartoon engine, the same graphic language as the earlier films in the series.
Format: vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 35.2 s, for TikTok. Thai first-person voice-over as the boss pig (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural, pitched slightly down), Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of "ถ้าคุณเกิดเป็นหมู ?" (ตูบเล่า), retold in our own words with well-documented pig facts (FACTS.md).

## Structure
The boss pig answers "if you were born a pig…" with four facts, one picture each, matched to what the pig is doing:
hook (arms crossed: "don't think you're dirty") → 1 clean (mopping: separate sleeping and toilet areas; mud because pigs barely sweat) → 2 smart (thumbs-up: knows its name, learns like a dog, joystick experiments) → 3 fast (shopping cart dash: ~17 km/h, speed lines) → 4 nose (close-up on the snout, a truffle call-out) → recap (four hard cuts, one word each) → end (waving: "เกิดเป็นหมู ก็ไม่เลว!"), iris-out on the face.

## Camera
Each picture is a "cover" crop that the camera moves over with keyframed pushes and pans (face → mop, face → cart, a slow push to the snout); a shake on the cart dash; hard cuts on the recap; medallion irises between facts; a colour-block wipe into the end.

## Sound
Synthesised cool-jazz swagger groove (walking bass, ride, finger snaps, flute licks on each tag), a chord per fact, a snare roll into a short silence before the recap, four stabs on the recap words. Foley: oink, mop squeaks, mud, game blips, cart wheels, sniffs, stamps, pops, sparkle. Voice ~10 dB over the music; −14 LUFS.
