# Facts in "ถ้าคุณเกิดเป็นหมู"

Retold in our own words from the topic of the short by ตูบเล่า (youtube.com/shorts/pHkeHxFHMhI); its captions could not be fetched.

| On screen | Basis |
|---|---|
| Pigs keep their sleeping area separate from their toilet area | Well documented in pig behaviour literature; pigs given space choose a dunging area away from where they lie and eat. |
| Pigs barely sweat; they wallow in mud to cool down and protect their skin from the sun | Pigs have very few functional sweat glands; wallowing is thermoregulation and sun protection. |
| Pigs learn their names and learn about as fast as dogs | Common findings in animal-cognition reviews (e.g. Marino & Colvin 2015, "Thinking Pigs"). |
| Researchers taught pigs to play a video game with a joystick | Croney et al., Frontiers in Psychology (2021); earlier work by Curtis at Penn State in the 1990s. |
| Pigs can run at about 17 km/h | Commonly cited figure (about 11 mph); stated as "ราว". |
| A sharp nose: pigs can sniff out truffles underground | Pigs have traditionally been used to hunt truffles. |
