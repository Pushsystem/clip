# Morihan Unryu — "มังกรเมฆ" · Treatment

Style: Tablet Sketch Explainer (`ipad-sketch`). 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, ~31 s, TikTok.
Language: Thai (voice th-TH-PremwadeeNeural, subtitles in Thai). Lettering on the page: Thai handwriting (Itim) + Latin marker hand (Caveat) for the product name; 雲龍 once in a Japanese brush-pen hand (Zen Kurenaido).

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **The name as a riddle.** Open on an empty page and a question: "มังกรเมฆ?" A single grey cloud-dragon line goes down and is left uncoloured. The page then answers the riddle in four drawings (can → shade-grown leaf → stone mill → cup), and at the end the dragon is re-registered matcha green: the name has "woken up". Ends on the whole page + the real photo taped on.
2. **From leaf to cup in one stroke.** A single long line followed by the stylus, from tea bush down the tall page to the latte glass. Strong vertical move, but turns the product into a generic matcha process film.
3. **Three tastes side by side.** The page split in three columns (floral / umami / seaweed). Good for the taste section, but no hook and no turn.

**Chosen: 1.** The name "Unryu = cloud dragon" is the most intriguing fact we have and costs nothing to say in 3 s, it gives a bookend (grey dragon → green dragon), and the vertical page fits a journey top→bottom.

## 2. Logline and arc

A matcha whose name means "cloud dragon": the page explains why it deserves the name, and the dragon turns green when the cup is done.
- Setup (0–8 s): the question, the dragon line, the can — Morihan, Uji, Kyoto.
- Development (8–16 s): first-flush leaf under shade → stone mill. Near-silence, then the leaf's green **shatters** into powder chips that pour down into the bowl (signature native move).
- Turn (16–23 s): what that powder tastes like — floral, sweet/umami, silky, seaweed finish — drawn as small notes + a four-chip swatch strip.
- Ending (23–31 s): pure (usucha bowl) or latte glass; pinch out to the whole page, the dragon is re-registered green, the real product photo is taped on, "กดตะกร้าได้เลย".

## 3. Benchmark

- Draw-along recipe explainers on tablets and time-lapse sketchbook reels on short-video platforms. **Learn:** the hook written on the page itself, one gesture per idea, notes landing next to the thing they name, a photo of the real thing at the end as "proof". **Don't take:** their drawings, lettering, characters, music or layouts.
- Japanese tea-house packaging illustration for colour restraint (deep green, warm cream, one red accent). **Don't take** any logos or marks.
