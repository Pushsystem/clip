# หมูตัวเดียวเกือบทำสงคราม — Treatment

Style: Mid-century Cartoon (`midcentury-toon`), third film in the same series. Vertical 1080×1920, 24 fps, 46.5 s, for TikTok. Thai voice-over (edge-tts th-TH-NiwatNeural) and Thai captions only.
Source: the topic of the short "หมูตัวเดียว เกือบทำสหรัฐฯ-อังกฤษรบกัน!" (ทุกโลกมีเรื่อง), the 1859 Pig War on San Juan Island, retold in our own words; its captions could not be fetched (FACTS.md).

## Structures considered
1. A straight chronicle: accurate but flat.
2. Two sides trading blows panel by panel: good, but needs more screen time than TikTok allows.
3. **Chosen**: a five-round "escalation card" (ยกที่ 1–5): the pig in the garden → $10 vs $100 → the build-up of troops and ships, with counters → the admiral who will not fire → the verdict. Opening: the pig between closing colour blocks of the two sides; ending: the pig as the only casualty, with a halo, then an iris-out.

## Shots (s)
0–3.2 two colour blocks (ships above, cannons below) close on a pig · 3.2–8.0 stylised map (not to scale), two dashed claimed lines (blue: the American claim, red: the British) · 8.0–12.6 round 1: the pig digs a potato patch, the farmer aims, "ปัง!" starburst with a camera shake, pig on its back · 12.6–15.5 round 2: split frame, coins "$10" / "$100" · 15.5–27.2 round 3: split frame sea/shore; ships sail in (3, then 5), a soldier grid fills, the counter runs 64 → 461, 14 cannons, "2,140" · silence 27.15–27.55 · 27.2–33.0 round 4: the British admiral raises his hand, ✗ over the warship, bubble "สงครามเพราะหมู? ไม่เอาด้วย!" · 33.0–36.6 tents of both sides on the island, the year counter 1860 → 1872 · 36.6–41.4 round 5: the decision paper "1872", the stamp "อเมริกา", the island turns blue · 41.4–46.5 the pig with wings and a halo, "ผู้เสียชีวิต: 1 (หมู)", iris-out.
No flags or real portraits: the two sides are blue and red; characters are generic.

## Sound
Synthesised cool-jazz combo; a march that climbs a step at every escalation; music stops dead on the shot; near-silence before the admiral; celesta ticks with the years; a stamp hit on the verdict; a music-box requiem for the pig. Foley: rifle cock and shot, coins, marching feet, ship horns, cannon clunks, ticks, harp. Voice ~10 dB over music, ducking 8 dB; −14 LUFS.
