# Facts in "หมูมาเอง"

The film is a light retelling of the topic of the short "ทำไมมนุษย์โบราณ... ถึงรู้ว่าหมูกินได้?!" (อิหยังวะศาสตร์ EP.39, youtube.com/shorts/9KE9y-JwyW4). Its captions could not be fetched; the script and the facts below are our own, from general scientific consensus. Figures are rounded and marked "ราว / ~" on screen.

| Claim on screen | Basis |
|---|---|
| Our ancestors have eaten meat for more than 2 million years ("กินเนื้อมา 2 ล้านปี+") | Cut-marked animal bones with stone tools appear by ~2.6–2.0 million years ago (e.g. Gona, Ethiopia; Kanjera South, Kenya). |
| Edible vs. not was learned by trying it and remembering ("อะไรกินแล้วไม่ตาย ก็จำต่อกันมา") | A simplification of trial-and-error learning and cultural transmission of food knowledge; told as a joke, not a citation. |
| Wild boar were hunted for tens of thousands of years ("หลายหมื่นปี") | Wild boar (Sus scrofa) bones are common in Palaeolithic hunting sites across Eurasia. |
| About 10,000 years ago people settled into villages and pigs were domesticated (Anatolia / Upper Mesopotamia) | Pig domestication is dated to ~10,500–10,000 years ago in south-eastern Anatolia (e.g. Çayönü). |
| Boars came to village rubbish by themselves; the tamer ones stayed ("commensal pathway") | The commensal pathway hypothesis (M. Zeder and others) for pig domestication. |
| The same happened independently in China about 9,000 years ago | Independent domestication of pigs in China, ~9,000–8,000 years ago (e.g. Jiahu). |
